Privacy policy

App: Nuvio w/ Live TV (Android TV / Google TV)
Last updated: September 28, 2026

Summary

The developer of this app does not run any server that stores your data, and does not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you turn on Google Drive sync, the app asks for permission to use a private app data folder in your own Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). It cannot see, open, or change any other files in your Drive.

The app saves one file in that folder containing your Live TV setup: playlist and guide (EPG) addresses, Xtream Codes logins you entered, favorites, hidden channels and groups, channel names and numbers you changed, and your Live TV settings. This is used only to copy your setup between your own devices.

The app also reads your Google account's email address, only to show which account is connected.

Your Google sign-in is stored only on your device. The developer never receives it, and never receives or stores the contents of your backup.

Removing your data

  • In the app: Settings → Live TV → Google Drive sync → Disconnect.
  • To delete the backup and remove access: open Google Drive on a computer → Settings (gear) → Manage apps → find the app → Options → Delete hidden app data, then Disconnect from Drive. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Other services

Playlists, guides, addons, and accounts you add (for example Trakt or your IPTV provider) are contacted directly from your device and are covered by those services' own privacy policies.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
